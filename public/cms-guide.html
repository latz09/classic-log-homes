Getting Started

Getting Started

Logging In

Your content management system (the "Studio") is where you edit everything on your website. To get in:

  1. Go to your Studio: classic-log-homes.sanity.studio
  2. Log in with the account you were invited with (Google, GitHub, or email/password).
  3. You'll land on Manage Content — your list of everything on the site, down the left side.
💡 Tip: Bookmark the Studio so you can jump back in fast: classic-log-homes.sanity.studio

How Manage Content Works

Everything happens in one place: Manage Content. The left side lists your content — single pages like Home Page, and collections like Floor Plans and Testimonials. Open a list, pick the item you want (or add a new one), fill in the fields, and hit Publish. That's the whole loop.

Drafts vs. Published

Every change you make is saved as a draft automatically. Drafts are not visible on the live website — only you can see them in the Studio.

To make changes go live, click the Publish button at the bottom right of the editor. Changes appear on your site within about a minute.

💡 Tip: You can't break the live site by editing. Your drafts stay private until you hit Publish. Edit freely, then publish when it looks right.

Working with Images

To add or change any image:

  1. Click the image field.
  2. Click "Upload" to pick a file from your computer, or drag and drop it in.
  3. Set a hotspot — click the image and drag the circle over the most important part (the cabin, the logs, the face). This keeps the image cropping correctly on phones and tablets.
  • Use JPG or PNG. The site converts and optimizes them automatically.
  • Real photos of Jeff's work beat stock every time.
  • Keep files reasonably sized (under ~4MB) so uploads stay quick.
  • Always set the hotspot on hero and plan photos.

Publishing

  1. Make your edits.
  2. Click "Publish" at the bottom right.
  3. Wait about a minute, then check the live site. If the change doesn't show, do a hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R on PC or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac.

Form Submissions

Every message sent through the website's contact form lands here, newest first. These are read-only — the real notifications come straight to your email. This list is just a backup record you can browse.

What each entry shows Name, Email, Phone Number, the Floor Plan they selected, their Message, Project Type (owner-builder / contractor / not sure), and the date & time it was sent. Suspected bot submissions are flagged with a 🚫 so you can ignore them.
🔐 About the 🚫 flag (spam protection): The form has a hidden "trap" field that real visitors never see. People leave it blank; automated spam bots fill in every field, so they fall right into it. When that happens, the entry is quietly logged here with a 🚫 and no email is sent to you — so your inbox only ever gets real inquiries. You don't need to do anything with the 🚫 entries; they're just kept for the record. If a real message ever looks flagged by mistake, let Jordan know.
⚠️ Note: Submissions can't be edited or deleted from the Studio. Manage real inquiries from the email notifications you receive.

Quick Tips

✅Always publishDrafts stay private. Nothing goes live until you hit Publish.
🎯Set hotspotsOn every image. Drag the circle over the subject so it crops right on phones.
🔗Pick, don't retypeFeatured plans & reviews pull from Floor Plans / Testimonials. Edit the source, not the home page.
➕Add plans anytimePublish a new Floor Plan and its page builds itself. Renaming a live plan changes its URL, so set the name before you share it.
⏱️Give it a minuteChanges take ~1 minute to appear. Hard refresh if needed: Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R.
✂️Keep text tightShort, clear copy converts. Say it and move on.
📱Yellow vs. redYellow length hints still publish. Red errors must be fixed first.
🧭Only edit nav labelsIn Navigation, change the Label text only — the URLs are locked to the page.
